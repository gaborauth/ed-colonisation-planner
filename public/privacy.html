Privacy

What this app does and doesn't collect, and how the cookie consent choice works. Linked from the cookie banner shown on first visit. See also the project's README for the full feature list.

What the app itself does

EDCPS is a client-only, stateless web app — there's no backend, no account, and no server this app runs that your data could be sent to. Everything you enter (system layout, saved plans, imported journal data) stays in your browser's own local storage, on your own device, unless you explicitly export a file yourself. The solver itself runs entirely in your browser too.

Google Analytics (opt-in only)

The one exception to the above: this site can optionally use Google Analytics to see aggregate usage stats (which pages get visited, roughly how often) — nothing this app collects for its own features (system layouts, saved plans, etc.) is ever included.

It's off by default. Nothing is requested from Google's servers, and no analytics cookie is set, until you click "Accept" on the cookie banner. If you click "Decline," or simply close the banner without choosing, nothing loads — same as if Analytics didn't exist on this site at all.

Once accepted, Google Analytics may set cookies to distinguish returning visits. Roughly:

  • _ga — distinguishes users, ~2 years
  • _ga_<container-id> — persists session state, ~2 years

See Google's own privacy policy and Analytics data disclosure for what Google itself does with this data.

Changing your mind

A small "Cookie settings" link stays visible in the bottom-left corner of the app at all times once you've made a choice — click it to reopen the banner and change your answer (either direction) at any time. Declining after previously accepting doesn't retroactively delete cookies Google has already set in your browser; clear them yourself via your browser's own cookie settings if you want that too.
